These programs are off the air on every station, and other programs play in their place. Missing files are gone from disk or from Plex while a station still has them. Broken files won’t play. Damaged files play, but the picture or sound breaks up. Unsupported files play but can’t be shown correctly (Dolby Vision profile 5). Replace them with another version. Fix or replace the file, then choose Retry. Or let the list clear itself: a program comes off the list when Plex has it again under a new ID (after a file is renamed, for example), or when it’s missing and no station has it anymore. (Removed it on purpose? Take it off the stations its entry lists.) A program with a new file, or one the quick check took off the air, comes off once it passes the quick check. Problems found by the deep scan or during playback stay until the file changes. Retry also tells the checks to leave that file on the air.

When people watch

A viewing counts once it lasts a minute, so flipping past a station doesn’t count. StationPlay counts the streams Plex asks it for, so several people watching one station together in Plex (or Plex recording it) may count as one viewing; each of StationPlay’s apps counts on its own. Stats are kept for 400 days. Today means since midnight.

Access

Admin
Can do everything: manage every station and use Stats, Add to Plex (including backups), Broken files, Logs, and Access (who can sign in).
User
Can watch the stations and library their Viewing Level allows (Unrestricted, with no limits, to start), and see Stats. Can make new stations (as many as you allow below, 3 to start) and change or delete only the stations they made. Can add logos (uploaded, or a show’s logo from Plex) and Intro Bumper videos, but only Admins can delete them. On a Viewing Level with limits, they only watch. Can’t open Add to Plex, Broken files, Logs, or Access.

Adding StationPlay to Plex
  1. In Plex, open Settings → Live TV & DVR → Set Up Plex DVR.
  2. Choose Don’t see your HDHomeRun device? Enter its network address manually (Plex can’t find StationPlay on its own) and enter the tuner address above.
  3. When Plex asks for guide data, choose Have an XMLTV guide on your server? Click here to use it and enter the guide address for Plex, shown above.
  4. Plex matches your stations automatically (it calls them channels). Continue, and they appear in the Live TV guide in Plex.

StationPlay keeps the Plex guide up to date on its own: when a station changes, it asks Plex to refresh the guide. If it can’t (see Guide updates above), refresh the guide in Plex after you change a station: Settings → Live TV & DVR → your tuner → Refresh Guide. For a new station, also choose Scan for channels there so Plex adds it.

Watching in Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi, or another app
  • Jellyfin: Dashboard → Live TV → Tuner Devices + → M3U Tuner, with the playlist address above. Then TV Guide Data Providers + → XMLTV, with the guide address for other apps.
  • Emby (needs Emby Premiere): Settings → Live TV → add a TV source → M3U Tuner, with the playlist address. Then add a guide data provider → XMLTV, with the guide address for other apps.
  • Kodi: Install the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on. In its settings, set M3U play list URL to the playlist address and XMLTV URL to the guide address for other apps.
  • Other apps (Channels DVR, TiviMate, VLC…): Give them the playlist address. It tells them where to find the guide.

These apps refresh their guide on their own schedule (about once a day). After you change a station, refresh the guide in the app to see the change right away.

Picture and tuners

Backups

Every night, StationPlay backs up your stations, settings, the Broken files list, your logos (uploaded or from Plex), who can sign in, and the viewing stats. It keeps the last 7 backups in the backups folder inside its data folder. To keep backups small, Intro Bumper videos aren’t included; they stay in the bumpers folder. Restoring a backup replaces all of this with the backup’s copy, and StationPlay restarts to finish.

Stations from Plex collections

Each collection you pick becomes a shuffled station named after the collection, with the Collection logo. You can change any of this later. Each station stays in sync with its collection: anything added to the collection in Plex joins the station within an hour. Collections with the same name in different libraries each get their own station, numbered like Sunshine1 and Sunshine2.
