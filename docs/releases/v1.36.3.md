A hotfix: setup's Tuner step, marking a whole show at once, and Resume starting 15 seconds back.

## New

- **The Tuner step.** Setup's Plex step is now called Tuner. It asks which apps you'll watch your stations in: Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi, another app, or only tapiOca's own apps. Then it shows the steps for each one. It only changes what the step shows. Every app can still use the tuner.
- **Mark a whole show** watched or unwatched in one step, in tapiOca's apps (Android 0.6.2 now; Apple and Roku next). The whole show is marked, or none of it is.
- **Resume starts 15 seconds back**, so you pick up the thread. This works in every app, even ones not yet updated.

## Fixed

- **A season left half marked.** Apps marked a season one episode at a time, so trouble partway through left it half marked. tapiOca's apps now mark a season in one step, and the whole season is marked or none of it is.
- **What 1.36.2's notes said of NIST.** They said its guidelines ask for 12 characters. NIST asks for at least 15 where a password is the only way to sign in. The minimum is still 12, and the README says so plainly now.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.3.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes. After updating, setup asks the Tuner step's question once.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.2` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.2 runs on 1.36.3's database as it is. Apps use one request per episode again with 1.36.2, and Resume starts exactly where you stopped.
