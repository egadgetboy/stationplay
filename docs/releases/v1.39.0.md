A Station you allow for someone by hand now pauses for them while something above their limits is coming up on it, and comes back once that has aired.

## New

- **Stations allowed by hand follow limits again** (your D01, October 17). Say you allow Saturday Toons for Kid, and a TV-14 episode is scheduled. Kid can't tune in from the time it shows up in the next 48 hours (the guide's window) until it has aired. Then Saturday Toons is back. The Access tab says until when, under **What they see**.
- **Someone already watching is stopped** when a schedule change or a new level means they can't watch that Station any more. Everyone else watches on, as one shared stream.

## Fixed

- **A guide asked for days ahead could show a limited viewer what's on a Station allowed for them** beyond what's been checked for them. Their guide now stops 48 hours ahead for those Stations, and the page leaves out what they're made of.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.39.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. Stations you allowed by hand may now be blocked for someone for a while, as above. Stations that follow a person's level are as before.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.38.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back, and nothing is lost.
