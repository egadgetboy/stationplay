Full or Light sign-in for each Admin, a sign-in screen that lists only your own Admins, people on a device only once they sign in there, and no Admin tabs before tapiOca says so.

## New

- **Full or Light sign-in,** each Admin's own choice in **Your account** (your name at the top of the page). **Full**, the default: your password for Admin tasks every time. **Light**: your password once on each browser or device, then your PIN there, at home, gives your Admin tools. Changing it takes your password.
- **Remove from this browser,** on the sign-in screen, takes an Admin off that browser's list.

## Fixed

- **The sign-in screen listed people who'd never signed in there,** Users too. It now lists only the Admins who signed in on that browser with their password. **Sign in with a password** is there for anyone.
- **An Admin's PIN on a trusted network hid the Admin tabs** with no word of why. In Light sign-in it now gives your Admin tools; otherwise a note says the sign-in is for watching, with **Sign in with your password**.
- **The Admin tabs showed for a moment** before tapiOca said who was signed in. Now nothing only an Admin may use shows until it does.
- **Apps listed people on Who's tuning in? who'd never signed in on that device.** Now each person signs in once on each device, with a password, an invite code or **Link an app**. **Show on** is gone from the Access tab.
- **A new role now ends that person's sign-ins,** so they sign in again as what they are now.
- **From 1.37.1's checks:** **Check files** pressed while a new station's first day was checked now checks everything. A check that failed to start, or a deleted station's check, no longer runs on. Setup's tuner address is the one Plex reaches, and only a plain machine address. A failing station's alert says when its failures began.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.38.0.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. The database changes, so tapiOca saves a copy first (`backups/before-1.38.0-<date>.db`). Every Admin starts on **Full**: to use your PIN for Admin tasks, choose **Light** in **Your account**. In the apps, people who were listed by **Show on** sign in once on each device. For someone with neither a password nor a PIN (Kids), make an invite code under **Devices**.

## Rolling back

Stop tapiOca, put back `backups/before-1.38.0-<date>.db` as `stationplay.db` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)), and use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.37.1`. Changes made since the update are lost, and everyone signs in again.
