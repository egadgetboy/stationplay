**New: copies converted on the GPU.**

- **Converting on the GPU.** When your stations use a GPU (Intel or AMD through VA-API, or NVIDIA), copies made for StationPlay's apps are now converted there too. That's light on the server's processor, so up to 6 can be converted at once instead of 3. Copies that are only repackaged don't change.
- **The processor behind it.** If a copy has trouble on the GPU, it carries on from the same point on the processor, with nothing more than the usual wait, just as stations do. If 3 copies in a row have trouble there, copies are converted on the processor until StationPlay restarts, and your stations keep the GPU.
- **Media.** On the Access tab, the setting that shares libraries with StationPlay's apps is now **Media in StationPlay's apps**, the name the apps use for that section.
- **In the apps.** StationPlay's apps, updated alongside, now tell StationPlay about problems as they happen (they show on the **Logs** tab, grouped by kind of device), night mode covers Media too, and on Android TV and Roku the title in focus shows on a TV set.

Without a GPU, copies are converted on the processor as before, 3 at once.

**Files.** `stationplay-1.25.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.25.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.25.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
