Copies for StationPlay's apps can now be converted on the GPU.

## New

- **Converting on the GPU.** When your stations use a GPU (Intel or AMD through VA-API, or NVIDIA), copies for the apps are converted there too, up to 6 at once instead of 3.
- **The processor as backup.** A copy that has trouble on the GPU continues on the processor from the same point. After 3 copies in a row have trouble, copies use the processor until StationPlay restarts, and stations keep the GPU.
- **Renamed setting.** The **Access** tab's setting for sharing libraries is now **Media in StationPlay's apps**, the name the apps use.

## Good to know

- **Without a GPU.** Copies are converted on the processor as before, 3 at once. Copies that are only repackaged don't change.
- **Updated apps.** The apps now report problems as they happen, grouped by kind of device on the **Logs** tab. Night mode covers Media too, and on Android TV and Roku the title in focus shows on a TV set.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.25.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.25.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.25.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
