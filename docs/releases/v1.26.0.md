**New: see that your apps can reach StationPlay from outside, what's playing, and how the server is doing.**

- **Ready for apps away from home.** While **StationPlay's apps away from home** is on, StationPlay checks its outside address the way an app would: on saving it, every 5 minutes, and with **Check now**. It confirms it reached itself over HTTPS through the public port, or says plainly what's wrong (the proxy points at port 3310, the certificate has expired, the proxy can't reach StationPlay, and so on). Admins see **Up**, **Down**, **Checking** or **Can't check** in the header on every tab, and the setup's **All set** says whether it's ready. Two failed checks in a row are needed for **Down**, and a router that won't let devices at home use the home's own address is never taken for an outage.
- **The address, without a port.** Behind a reverse proxy, the address apps use from outside is its `https://` address alone, such as `https://tv.example.com`.
- **What's playing, in the Logs.** Each program on a station and each title played from Media says which file it is, its picture and sound, and how it plays: as it is, repackaged, or converted (to what, on the GPU or the processor, and why). Any step down in quality is logged, with the reason, and stations are named everywhere ("station 2, Cartoon Classics").
- **Stats, much fuller.** Viewing in StationPlay's apps now counts, for each person. For Admins: the server's processor, memory, network, GPU and storage, live; who's watching now, on what, and how it's playing; the top people; and the most watched in Media, beside the most watched stations.
- **Signed out after an hour.** StationPlay's page signs out after an hour without activity, for Admins and Users. The apps stay signed in.
- **Visitors' real addresses.** Behind a reverse proxy such as NPMplus, the access log and the limits on wrong passwords now use each visitor's own address, not the proxy's.
- **Safer linked devices.** An Admin on a device someone else linked always gives their password, and many PIN guesses sent at once can no longer get past the limit of five.
- **The page as an app.** StationPlay's page fits phones, tablets and computers, in either orientation, light or dark. Add it to a home screen, or install it on a computer, and it opens in a window of its own.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.26.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.26.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
