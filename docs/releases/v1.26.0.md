StationPlay now shows whether your apps can reach it from outside, what's playing and how the server is doing.

## New

- **Reachable from outside.** While **StationPlay's apps away from home** is on, StationPlay checks that it can reach itself over HTTPS through the public port. It checks on saving, every 5 minutes and with **Check now**.
- **Clear status.** Admins see **Up**, **Down**, **Checking** or **Can't check** in the header, and setup's **All set** shows whether it's ready. A failed check shows why, such as a proxy pointing at port 3310 or an expired certificate.
- **What's playing, in the logs.** Each program and Media title logs its file, picture and sound, and whether it plays unchanged, repackaged or converted, and why. Any step down in quality is logged, and stations are named everywhere ("station 2, Cartoon Classics").
- **Fuller Stats.** Viewing in the apps now counts for each person. Admins also see live server use, who's watching what and how it's playing, the top people and the most watched in Media.
- **The page as an app.** StationPlay's page fits phones, tablets and computers, light or dark, and can be added to a home screen or installed in its own window.

## Fixed

- **Visitors' real addresses.** Behind a reverse proxy such as NPMplus, the access log and the limits on wrong passwords now use each visitor's own address.
- **Safer linked devices.** An Admin always enters their password on a device someone else linked. Many passcode guesses sent at once can no longer get past the limit of 5.

## Good to know

- **No false alarms.** **Down** needs two failed checks in a row. A router that won't let devices at home use the home's own address isn't taken for an outage.
- **An address without a port.** Behind a reverse proxy, apps use its `https://` address alone, such as `https://tv.example.com`.
- **Signed out after an hour.** StationPlay's page signs out after an hour without activity. The apps stay signed in.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.26.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.26.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.26.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
