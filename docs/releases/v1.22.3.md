This release fixes missing commercials on stations made just after StationPlay starts.

## Fixed

- **Commercials at startup.** A station made, changed or updated from Plex while StationPlay was still finding commercials and trailers got none until its next check of Plex. Now it waits for that first look to finish.
- **Two flaky tests.** A commercials test that sometimes failed on GitHub and a blocks test that failed late in the evening UTC are fixed. The full suite passes again.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.3.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.22.3.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.3` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
