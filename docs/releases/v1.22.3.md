**Fixed: a station made just after StationPlay starts gets its commercials.**

- While StationPlay was still looking for commercials and trailers as it started, a station made, changed or updated from Plex in those first moments was given none until StationPlay next checked Plex for updates. It now waits for that first look to finish.
- Tests: one that failed now and then on GitHub (the commercials) and one that failed only late in the evening, UTC (a block's), are fixed; the full suite passes again.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.3.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.22.3.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.3` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
