StationPlay now checks Media's files too, lets people report problems from the apps and gathers it all on the **Broken files** tab.

## New

- **Media's files checked.** The libraries you share with the apps are now checked too, with one **Broken files** list for stations and Media. New additions are checked within minutes, and the overnight deep scan checks the rest, starting with titles in someone's **Resume** row.
- **People report problems.** From a title's page, the player's menu or a station's player, people pick what's wrong from a list, such as no picture, sound out of sync or the wrong episode. There's nothing to type.
- **Checked or left to you.** StationPlay checks what it can right away, such as no picture or sound. The rest wait for you, with **Replace with Sonarr** (or **Radarr**), **Find a better copy** or **Dismiss**.
- **The Broken files tab.** It now has **Needs you**, **Being replaced** and **Found by StationPlay**, and its count is what needs you.
- **An alert for Admins.** At most once an hour, a new Admin alert gives the count and the newest ("There are 3 files to look at on the Broken files tab: Tia reported No sound on Northbound S2 E4.").

## Good to know

- **Each file checked once.** A file checked for a station isn't checked again for Media, or the other way around, unless it changes. Sonarr and Radarr replace Media's files with the same settings.
- **Checks pause for viewers.** File checks now pause while anyone watches a station or Media, not only a station.
- **StationPlay's own findings count.** When an app reports trouble, StationPlay checks the file itself. Only what StationPlay finds puts a file on the list.
- **Broken files skipped in Media.** Another version plays if there's a good one. Otherwise the app shows "This one can't play right now. An Admin has been told." A damaged file still plays.
- **Report limits.** Reports never take anything off the air by themselves. Each person can send one report a day about each program and 10 a day in all, and **Can report problems** on the **Access** tab (on by default) turns them off for someone.
- **Updated apps.** Reporting a problem and offering another version come with the apps' next releases. Until then, StationPlay plays another version for the apps too.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.30.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.30.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.30.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings and sign-ins are kept, and so is your Plex token. As it starts, 1.30.0 saves a copy of the database in `data/backups` as `before-1.30.0-<date>.db`, then updates it. Media's files are checked from then on, new additions as they arrive and the rest at night. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.29.1 (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. In the data folder, replace `stationplay.db` with `backups/before-1.30.0-<date>.db`, and delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm` if they're there.
3. Unzip `stationplay-1.29.1.zip` over `src`.
4. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.29.1`, and start it.

Everyone stays signed in, and everything is as it was just before the update. Anything changed since is lost, including people's reports. The **Broken files** list stays as 1.30.0 left it. 1.29.1 uses the stations' entries as before and ignores those for a program's other versions, which only Media plays.
