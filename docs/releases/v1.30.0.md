**New: one process for every file, the stations' and Media's, people's reports from the apps, and the Broken files tab that holds it all.**

- **One process for every file.** StationPlay's file checks now cover Media (the libraries you share with StationPlay's apps), not only the stations' programs, with one Broken files list for both: a file found broken for a station is broken in Media too, and the other way around, and a file checked for one isn't checked again for the other unless it changes. What's newly added to a shared library is checked within minutes, after the stations' new programs; the overnight deep scan, after the stations' programs, scans Media's files in full: what's in someone's **Resume** row first, then the next episode of a show someone is watching, then the rest, newest first. File checks pause while anyone is watching, a station or Media (before, only a station counted). Sonarr and Radarr replace Media's files as they do the stations', with the same settings.
- **Trouble playing a file is checked, never taken as a verdict.** When a copy for an app can't be made or stops, or an app says something didn't play or stopped, StationPlay checks that file first, around where it happened, then gives it the quick check. Only what StationPlay itself finds puts a file on the list: trouble that was the network's or the app's changes nothing.
- **What's broken doesn't play in Media.** Another version of it plays instead, if there's one StationPlay didn't find broken, and the app says so; with none, the app shows "This one can't play right now. An Admin has been told." A damaged file still plays, as before.
- **People report problems from the apps.** From a movie's or an episode's page, the player's menu, or a station's player, people pick what's wrong from a list (no picture, the sound out of sync, wrong language, subtitles, wrong episode, and more); there's nothing to type. What StationPlay can check (no picture or sound, the picture breaking up, the sound cutting out, stopping early, not playing) is checked at once, where it happened first. What only a person can judge waits for you, with what StationPlay can tell beside it (the file's sound languages, its length, its picture's size, its subtitles): **Replace with Sonarr** (or **Radarr**), **Find a better copy**, or **Dismiss**. A report never takes anything off the air or out of Media by itself. Each person can send one report a day about each program, and ten a day in all, and **Can report problems** on the **Access** tab turns it off for someone (it's on for everyone to start with).
- **The Broken files tab holds it all.** It now has three parts: **Needs you** (people's reports, and files a station or Media plays that are waiting for you), **Being replaced** (what Sonarr or Radarr is fetching now), and **Found by StationPlay** (the rest). Several reports on one program are one row, each under it, saying who sent it, from which device, where in the program and how it was playing. Each file says where it's used: on which stations, and in Media. The tab's count is what needs you, and the settings for reports sit beside Sonarr's and Radarr's.
- **Told when something needs you.** A new Admin alert says when something on the **Broken files** tab needs you, how many, and the newest ("There are 3 files to look at on the Broken files tab: Tia reported No sound on Northbound S2 E4."), at most once an hour, in the page's header, the apps and at your web address, as other alerts are.
- **In the apps.** Report a problem, and offering another version of something that can't play, come with the apps' next releases. Until then, the apps play as before: StationPlay plays another version for them too, and they show its refusal as it's sent.

**Files.** `stationplay-1.30.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.30.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.30.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings and sign-ins are kept, and so is your Plex token. As it starts, 1.30.0 saves a copy of the database in `data/backups` as `before-1.30.0-<date>.db`, then updates it. Media's files are checked from then on: what's added after the update as it arrives, and the rest at night. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

**Rolling back.** To go back to 1.29.1 (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. In the data folder, replace `stationplay.db` with `backups/before-1.30.0-<date>.db`, and delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm` if they're there.
3. Unzip `stationplay-1.29.1.zip` over `src`.
4. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.29.1`, and start it.

Everyone stays signed in, and everything is as it was just before the update; anything changed since is lost, people's reports included. The Broken files list stays as 1.30.0 left it: 1.29.1 goes by the stations' entries as before, and passes over those for a program's other versions, which only Media plays.
