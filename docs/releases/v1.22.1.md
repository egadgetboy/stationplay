StationPlay's apps can now send problem reports to the **Logs** tab.

## New

- **Send a report.** In an app, choose **Options → Send a report to StationPlay**. It sends what the app did recently, the device and what it can play, the server's version and any crash.
- **Nothing private.** A report never includes a password, a token or a stream's private address.
- **Copy to pass along.** The **Logs** tab shows the report unchanged, and **Copy** copies it for whoever is looking into the problem.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.22.1.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
