**Problem reports from StationPlay's apps.**

- In an app, **Options → Send a report to StationPlay** puts what the app did lately, the device and what it can play, the server's version, and any crash into StationPlay's **Logs** tab, for whoever is looking into a problem. A report never includes a password, a token or a stream's private address.
- The Logs tab shows a report's lines as they are; **Copy** copies it to pass along.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.22.1.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
