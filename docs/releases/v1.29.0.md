This release adds languages that follow each person, Admin alerts, burn-in protection and "passcode" on the page.

## New

- **Languages that follow you.** Each person chooses a sound language and subtitles in an app's Options, and StationPlay keeps them on every device. In the player, they can choose differently for a whole show or for one episode or movie.
- **Admin alerts.** StationPlay alerts you when Plex can't be reached, the data folder is nearly full or can't be written to, a station keeps failing to start, backups fail, the clock is badly off from Plex's, or the apps can't reach it from outside. An alert appears once a problem persists, and again when it's fixed.
- **Where alerts show.** Alerts show on the **Logs** tab and, for Admins, in the apps. A count in the page's header opens the list.
- **Alerts to a web address.** At the top of the **Logs** tab, have each alert sent to your web address when it starts and when it's fixed. Use **Plain text** for an ntfy topic, or **JSON** for Gotify, Home Assistant and others, and **Send a test** to try it.
- **Burn-in protection.** Each program starts with the corner logo, name or clock moved to the next of 9 places within 6 pixels of its corner, so an OLED or plasma TV never has the same pixels lit for hours. There's nothing to set.
- **"Passcode" everywhere.** The **Access** tab and StationPlay's messages now say passcode instead of PIN, as the apps do.

## Good to know

- **Which track plays.** The episode's or movie's choice comes first, then the show's, then the person's, then the file's default. Commentary never plays when there's another track, and with subtitles off, only forced subtitles show.
- **No choice, no change.** Someone who hasn't chosen anything gets each file as before, and stations don't change.
- **The web address stays private.** Sending alerts is off by default. Only Admins see the address, and the log names only its site.
- **Updated apps.** Language choices, **Remember for the whole show** and alerts for Admins come with the apps' own updates (StationPlay for Android 0.3.0).

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.29.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.29.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.29.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
