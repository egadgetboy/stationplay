**New: languages that follow each person, Admin alerts (sent to a web address if you like), burn-in protection, and "passcode" on the page too.**

- **Languages.** Each person chooses their own sound language and captions (on or off, and in which language) in an app's Options, and StationPlay keeps them, so they follow that person to every device. From the player, they can choose otherwise for a whole show, or for one episode or movie. When something plays from your library, StationPlay picks its sound and subtitles from that: the episode's or movie's choice, then the show's, then their own, then the file's default. The sound comes in their language (never a commentary when there's another), captions in theirs (a full track before a forced one), and with captions off, only forced subtitles, for the parts in another language. Subtitles a device can't show itself are drawn in. Someone who hasn't chosen anything gets each file as before, and stations don't change.
- **Admin alerts.** StationPlay now says when something needs a look: Plex can't be reached, the data folder is nearly full or can't be written to, a station keeps failing to start, backups fail, the clock is badly off (compared with Plex's), or StationPlay's apps can't reach it from outside. Each is said once it has lasted a while, and again when it's fixed, so none comes and goes: in the **Logs** tab, and for Admins, in a pill in the page's header that says how many there are and opens a list of them. Admins see them in StationPlay's apps too.
- **Notify a web address.** At the top of the **Logs** tab, have each alert sent to a web address of yours when it starts and when it's fixed: an ntfy topic (**Plain text**), or Gotify, Home Assistant and others (**JSON**). **Send a test** tries it at once. It's off to start, only Admins see the address, and the log names only its site.
- **Burn-in protection.** Each program on a station starts with its corner logo, name or clock moved a little, to the next of 9 places within 6 pixels of the corner you chose, so a screen that keeps a still picture (an OLED or plasma TV) never has the same pixels lit for hours. It works the same on a GPU, the Up Next Banner still lands exactly on the logo, and there's nothing to set.
- **"Passcode" everywhere.** The **Access** tab and StationPlay's messages say passcode where they said PIN, as the apps do.
- **In the apps.** Audio language and Captions in Options, Remember for the whole show in the player, and alerts for Admins come with the apps' own updates (StationPlay for Android 0.3.0).

**Files.** `stationplay-1.29.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.29.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.29.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
