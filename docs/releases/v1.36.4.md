A hotfix: INFO plays again.

## Fixed

- **INFO said "We'll be right back."** Since 1.35.0, tapiOca looked for INFO's film in your media folder, because its name looks like one of your own Media folders' titles. It was never there, so station 99 showed its card instead. tapiOca now finds the film where it keeps it.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.4.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.3` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.3 runs on 1.36.4's database as it is, and INFO shows its card again.
