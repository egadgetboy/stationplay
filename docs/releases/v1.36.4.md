A hotfix: INFO plays again, night mode cuts deep rumble, and the log says what really happened.

## Fixed

- **INFO said "We'll be right back."** Since 1.35.0, tapiOca has looked for INFO's film among your own files, because INFO's inner name looks like a Media folder's. The film was never there, so station 99 showed its "We'll be right back" card. tapiOca now finds the film where it keeps it.
- **Night mode let deep rumble through.** It now cuts sound under 50 Hz, steeply, so explosions don't shake the room or pull the voices down. Bass from 100 Hz up is as it was. This is the sound tapiOca makes for Apple's and Roku's apps, and for Media copies made with night mode. tapiOca for Android 0.6.3 makes it the same way on the device.
- **The log got three things wrong.** A program that played to its end said "stopped": it says "finished" now. Going from a converted copy back to the file itself said "switched to a smaller version… to fit its connection": it isn't smaller, so it's said as an ordinary start, and the connection is named only when it's why. And a copy with subtitles drawn in now says which ones, and why.
- **Even sound's switch was hard to find.** It's in setup's "Media in tapiOca's apps" step now too, as well as on the Access tab. Stations always have even sound; the switch is for Media.
- **The browser's tab** showed the old icon from before tapiOca's name in some browsers. It shows tapiOca's mark, and says "tapiOca Server".

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.4.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.3` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.3 runs on 1.36.4's database as it is. INFO then shows its "We'll be right back" card again, and night mode lets rumble through.
