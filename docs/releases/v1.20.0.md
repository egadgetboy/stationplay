**How many can watch at once in StationPlay's apps.**

- On the **Access** tab, an Admin can limit how many devices watch through StationPlay's apps (and other HLS players) at once, and how many of those away from home. A device already watching can always change stations; one more is told why, plainly, and asked to try again later.
- **Connection tests:** the apps time how fast StationPlay reaches them, at home and away from home. StationPlay keeps the results, recommends limits from the fastest recent tests, and warns when a limit is set above them. No outside speed-test service is used.
- The page warns when the tuners are set above what the server's own speed test found.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.20.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.20.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.20.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
