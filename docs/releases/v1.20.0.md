An Admin can now limit how many devices watch through StationPlay's apps at once.

## New

- **Limits on devices watching.** On the **Access** tab, limit how many devices watch at once through StationPlay's apps and other HLS players. A second limit covers devices away from home.
- **A clear message.** A device already watching can always change stations. One more device sees why it can't watch and is asked to try again later.
- **Connection tests.** The apps measure how fast StationPlay reaches them, at home and away, with no outside speed-test service. StationPlay recommends limits from the fastest recent tests and warns when a limit is above them.
- **Tuner warning.** StationPlay's page warns when the tuners are set above what the server's own speed test found.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.20.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.20.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.20.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
