**New: Media away from home, even sound for a show's episodes, and renaming people.**

- **Media away from home.** With **StationPlay's apps away from home** on, your library plays in StationPlay's apps through the public port too, as your stations already do: browsing, search, details, Resume and playing, for everyone signed in, within their Viewing Level and your limits on devices watching (away from home too). Each title an app plays there has a private address of its own that works only over HTTPS, only for a title started there, and only while that app's sign-in lasts.
- **Quality away from home.** On the **Access** tab, beside the outside address: **Media away from home** plays each title as it would at home (**Original**), or **Up to** the number of Mbps you choose. A title over it is converted down to fit; one within it plays as it would at home. Your home's upload, as StationPlay's apps measured it from outside, shows beside it as a guide.
- **Even sound for a show's episodes.** Episodes played from your library now come at the same loudness as the episodes on your stations, so a show's episodes match, in order or shuffled, and none is much louder or quieter than the next. Movies are never changed. Only the sound is made again; the picture plays as it is. It's on to start: turn it off under **Media in StationPlay's apps** to play each episode's sound as it is, such as Dolby Atmos or DTS sent on to a receiver.
- **Renaming people.** An Admin can rename anyone on the **Access** tab, themselves and other Admins too. People sign in with the new name from then on, and everything of theirs stays theirs: their sign-ins and linked devices, the stations they made, their Viewing Level, where they are in what they watch, and their stats. StationPlay's apps show the new name the next time they ask.
- **Safer linked devices.** A device is now its linker's by who they are, not by their name, so an Admin given the name of someone since removed never has that person's device for their own.

Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
