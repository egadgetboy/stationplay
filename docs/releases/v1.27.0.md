Media now works away from home, a show's episodes play at even loudness, and an Admin can rename people.

## New

- **Media away from home.** With **StationPlay's apps away from home** on, your library works in the apps through the public port, like your stations, within each person's Viewing Level and your limits on devices watching.
- **Private addresses.** Each title played away from home gets its own private address. It works only over HTTPS, only for that title and only while that app's sign-in lasts.
- **Quality away from home.** On the **Access** tab, set **Media away from home** to **Original** or **Up to** a number of Mbps. Larger titles are converted down, and your home's upload speed, as the apps measured it, shows as a guide.
- **Even sound for episodes.** Episodes from your library now play at the same loudness as on your stations, in order or shuffled. Movies are never changed.
- **Renaming people.** An Admin can rename anyone on the **Access** tab, including Admins. People keep their sign-ins, devices, stations, Viewing Level, progress and stats, and the apps show the new name the next time they connect.

## Fixed

- **Safer linked devices.** A device now belongs to the person who linked it, not to their name, so an Admin given a removed person's name never gets their device.

## Good to know

- **Turning off even sound.** Even sound is on by default. Turn it off under **Media in StationPlay's apps** to keep each episode's original sound, such as Dolby Atmos or DTS for a receiver.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.27.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.27.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.27.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
