The first public release under the AGPL plays movies kept in several files, keeps alerts across restarts and fixes stations on the FFmpeg in StationPlay's image.

## New

- **Open source under the AGPL.** StationPlay moves from the GPL-3.0 to the AGPL-3.0, copyright egadgetboy. A **Source** link at the foot of the page offers its code.
- **"PIN" everywhere,** instead of "passcode." **Set PIN** or **Change PIN** is next to **New password** in the **Access** tab's list of people.
- **Plainer device choices.** A person's **Devices** choices are now **Only devices they sign in on**, **Every device at home**, **Every device, at home and away** and **Only devices you choose**.
- **When a program ends.** Each progress bar on the **Stations** tab marks the program's end in orange, and the mark turns red as the program ends.

## Fixed

- **Movies in several files** ("cd1" and "cd2") play as one program, on stations and in Media. Before, only the first file played. File checks look at every part, and a missing or broken part puts the movie on the **Broken files** list, with a stand-in for the rest of its time.
- **Episodes and breaks with no sound** play again. On the image's FFmpeg, they stalled and were taken off the air.
- **Cards,** such as "We'll be right back," play smoothly. On the image's FFmpeg, they could come out late or stall.
- **Subtitles drawn on a station** no longer change its frame rate, which made such programs play at their file's own rate.
- **A damaged file** no longer leaves a short gap in the picture where its stand-in takes over.
- **Burn-in protection moves** to the next of its 9 places every 4 minutes, all through every program.
- **Alerts are kept across restarts,** so a restart doesn't send them again, and one your web address missed is sent once StationPlay is back.
- **The deep scan finds exact times** for damage. Files already listed keep their earlier times.
- **Tested as it ships.** A release and its image are published only once their tests pass in that image, which now includes the AGPL's text.

## Good to know

- **New people show only on devices they sign in on,** unless you chose **Devices at home** before (now **Every device at home**). **Use for everyone**, next to **New people show on**, applies the choice to everyone already added. Someone with no password and no PIN starts on no device's list until you choose theirs.
- **Someone who used to pick themselves on a device** signs in there once, with a password or an invite code.
- **Sonarr and Radarr can't replace one part** of a movie, so StationPlay asks you to replace it yourself.
- **Alerts going when you update** are reported once more, because 1.30.0 kept them only in memory.
- **Known problem:** on the image's FFmpeg, a program whose sound has a gap, or starts more than 3 seconds after its picture, can stall and be skipped. A fix is coming in 1.31.1.

## Updating

`stationplay-1.31.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`), `stationplay.yaml` is for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

Unzip `stationplay-1.31.0.zip` over `src`, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.31.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings, sign-ins and Plex token are kept. As it starts, 1.31.0 saves a copy of the database in `data/backups` as `before-1.31.0-<date>.db`, then updates it. Afterward, check **New people show on**, under **Linked devices** on the **Access** tab. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.30.0 (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. In the data folder, replace `stationplay.db` with `backups/before-1.31.0-<date>.db`, and delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm` if they're there.
3. Unzip your copy of `stationplay-1.30.0.zip` over `src` (releases before 1.31.0 are no longer on GitHub).
4. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.30.0`, and start it.

Everyone stays signed in, and everything is as it was just before the update. Anything changed since is lost.
