**New: a setup that walks you through everything, and checks that it works.**

- **First-time setup.** The first time an Admin opens StationPlay's page, the setup goes step by step: checking your server, how stations play, new station settings, who can use StationPlay, watching away from home, your library in StationPlay's apps, checking files, and adding StationPlay to Plex. It ends with what's set now, and anything that still needs a look. Each answer is saved when you go on to the next step.
- **Checks that say what to do.** StationPlay checks that it can reach Plex (and whether the account that owns your Plex server has Plex Pass, which watching in Plex needs), that it can read your media files from disk, how it encodes video, that its clock matches your browser's, and its backups. Anything marked **Needs a look** or **Not working** says how to fix it; **Check again** checks once more.
- **Run it again any time.** **Run setup again** on the **Add to Plex** tab, or **Setup** at the foot of the page, opens it with your current answers filled in.
- **After an update.** When a new version brings something that needs an Admin's answer, the setup opens once by itself with just those questions. Updating to 1.22.4 asks about watching away from home and your library in StationPlay's apps (unless they're already on), and about the settings new stations start with if you haven't been asked yet. Everything else stays as you set it.
- **Light or dark.** The page follows your device's setting. To keep it light or dark in one browser, choose **Light** or **Dark** under **Appearance** at the foot of the page.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.4.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.22.4.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.4` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
