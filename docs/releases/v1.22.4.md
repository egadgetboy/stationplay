A new setup walks you through StationPlay and checks that everything works.

## New

- **First-time setup.** The first time an Admin opens StationPlay's page, setup covers your server, how stations play, new station settings, who can use StationPlay, watching away from home, your library in the apps, checking files and adding StationPlay to Plex. Each answer is saved as you go.
- **Checks and fixes.** Setup checks Plex, Plex Pass (which watching in Plex needs), reading your media files from disk, video encoding, the clock against your browser's and backups. Anything marked **Needs a look** or **Not working** shows how to fix it, and **Check again** checks once more.
- **Run it again any time.** Use **Run setup again** on the **Add to Plex** tab, or **Setup** at the bottom of the page.
- **Light or dark.** The page follows your device's setting, or choose **Light** or **Dark** under **Appearance** at the bottom of the page.

## Good to know

- **Setup after an update.** When a new version needs an Admin's answer, setup opens once with just those questions. Updating to 1.22.4 asks about watching away from home, your library in the apps and new stations' settings, unless they're already set.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.4.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.22.4.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.4` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
