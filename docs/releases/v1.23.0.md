**New: who can watch what, and Who's tuning in? on the TVs everyone shares.**

- **Viewing Levels.** Each person has one. **Unrestricted** shows everything and stays that way; everyone starts on it. Every other level is yours: **Teen**, **Kid** and **Young Child** are there to start from, and you can rename, change or remove them, and add as many as you need, such as "Adults" with no R-rated movies or unrated titles. A level sets the highest movie and TV ratings shown (ratings from other countries are read too, such as 15 in the UK), whether unrated titles are shown, and which libraries can be seen. Change a level and it changes for everyone on it. Admins see everything.
- **Stations, all or nothing.** A station is shown to someone only if everything on it is within their level, or an Admin allowed that station for them. An Admin can also allow or block single stations for each person, under **Stations** on the Access tab, which says why each one is shown or hidden.
- **Hidden everywhere.** What someone can't see isn't in their guide, stations, library, search, Resume row or what's on now, in any app. StationPlay checks it on the server. (Watching through Plex isn't limited: Plex decides what its own users see.)
- **Watch only.** **None** is now a choice for how many stations a User can make. A User can also have no password, such as "Kids", who just picks themselves on the TV.
- **Who's tuning in?** Once StationPlay's apps are updated, signing in on a TV, phone or tablet links it, and from then on it opens on the people who use it. Each picks themselves, with a 4-digit PIN if they have one (5 wrong tries, then a 15-minute wait). An Admin gives their password on a device others use too. **Sign in** there takes a name and a password, or an invite code an Admin made (it works once, for 7 days).
- **Shown on which devices.** For each person, an Admin chooses **All devices**, **Selected devices**, or **Only where they sign in**, and the setting new people start with. Someone with neither a password nor a PIN is never signed in by name. **Linked devices** on the Access tab lists each device and who's on it, and unlinking one signs it out.
- **Night mode in every app.** Apple's and Roku's players can't change a station's sound themselves, so StationPlay makes night mode's sound beside each station, only while an app asks for it: loud scenes softer and dialogue easier to hear. It watches the station as one more viewer, so it needs no tuner of its own, and everyone else keeps the usual sound.
- **Problems in the apps.** Once StationPlay's apps are updated, they tell it about problems as they happen: a station that doesn't start or stops, something from your library that doesn't play, playing that can't keep up, or the app closing unexpectedly. At the top of the **Logs** tab, each problem shows how often it happened, on how many devices and for whom, and on what kinds of device, marked **One kind of device** when that's all it's been on, so you can tell one device's problem from everyone's.
- **Setup asks.** The setup has a step for who sees what, and updating to 1.23.0 asks about it once if there's more than one person. A StationPlay with one person works just as before.

**Files.** `stationplay-1.23.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.23.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.23.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
