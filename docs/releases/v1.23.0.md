Viewing Levels now control who can watch what, and shared TVs open on **Who's tuning in?**

## New

- **Viewing Levels.** A person's level sets the highest movie and TV ratings they see (other countries' too, such as 15 in the UK), whether unrated titles show and which libraries they see.
- **Levels you control.** Everyone starts on **Unrestricted**, which can't be changed. Rename, change or remove **Teen**, **Kid** and **Young Child**, or add your own, such as "Adults" with no R-rated movies or unrated titles.
- **Hidden everywhere.** What someone can't see is left out of their guide, stations, library, search, Resume row and what's on now, in every app. The server enforces this.
- **Stations, all or nothing.** A station shows only if everything on it fits the person's level, unless an Admin allows it for them under **Stations** on the **Access** tab.
- **Who's tuning in?** Once the apps are updated, a TV, phone or tablet you sign in on opens on the people who use it. Each picks themselves, with a 4-digit passcode if they have one.
- **Night mode in every app.** StationPlay makes night mode's sound itself for Apple's and Roku's players, with no extra tuner. Everyone else keeps the usual sound.
- **Problems from the apps.** Once updated, the apps report problems as they happen. The top of the **Logs** tab shows how often each happened, on how many devices, for whom and on what kinds of device.

## Good to know

- **Which devices show someone.** For each person, and for new people, choose **All devices**, **Selected devices** or **Only where they sign in**. **Linked devices** on the **Access** tab lists each device, and unlinking one signs it out.
- **Signing in on shared devices.** **Sign in** takes a name and a password, or an Admin's invite code that works once, for 7 days. An Admin always enters their password on a device others use too.
- **Passcode limits.** After 5 wrong tries, there's a 15-minute wait. Someone with neither a password nor a passcode is never signed in by name.
- **Watch-only Users.** **None** is now a choice for how many stations a User can make, and a User can have no password, such as "Kids", who just picks themselves.
- **Admins and Plex.** Admins see everything. Watching through Plex isn't limited, because Plex decides what its own users see.
- **Setup asks once.** With more than one person, updating to 1.23.0 asks once about who sees what. With one person, StationPlay works as before.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.23.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.23.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.23.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
