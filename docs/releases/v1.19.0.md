StationPlay's first public release turns your Plex library into broadcast-style TV stations for Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi and other IPTV apps.

## New

- **Stations from your library.** Pick shows and movies, a Plex collection or a filter, or let smart stations make one per decade, genre, studio and more.
- **Specials and binge-watching.** Marathons, Feature Presentations and blocks make specials, and a station can skip intros and credits.
- **One steady stream per station.** Each has commercials and trailers, an Intro Bumper, Station ID cards, an Up Next Banner and its logo in the corner.
- **Kept on the air.** Files are checked ahead of time. A file that won't play is skipped and listed on the **Broken files** tab, for Sonarr or Radarr to replace.
- **Ready for StationPlay's apps.** The apps, still in development, get the stations, the guide and HLS streams. They sign in on a TV with a code and work away from home through a reverse proxy with HTTPS.
- **Admins and Users.** Everyone signs in as an Admin or a User, with an access log, nightly backups and viewing stats.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.19.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.19.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.19.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
