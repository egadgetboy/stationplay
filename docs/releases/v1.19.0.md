StationPlay's first public release: broadcast-style TV stations made from your Plex library, for Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi and other IPTV apps.

- **Stations from your library:** pick shows and movies, a Plex collection or a filter, or make many at once with smart stations (one per decade, genre, studio and more). Marathons, Feature Presentations and blocks make specials, and a station can skip intros and credits for binge-watching.
- **One steady stream per station,** with commercials and trailers, an Intro Bumper, Station ID cards, an Up Next Banner and the station's logo in the corner.
- **Kept on the air:** files that won't play are skipped and listed on the Broken files tab, files are checked ahead of time, and Sonarr and Radarr can replace them.
- **For StationPlay's own apps** (in development): the stations, their guide and HLS streams; signing in with a code on a TV; and watching away from home through a reverse proxy with HTTPS.
- **Who can use it:** sign-in with Admins and Users, an access log, nightly backups, and viewing stats.

**Files.** `stationplay-1.19.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.19.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.19.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
