**Your library in StationPlay's apps.**

- Off until an Admin chooses which libraries to share, on the **Access** tab under **Your library in StationPlay's apps**. Stations, Plex, Jellyfin and other apps don't change either way.
- The apps browse and search your shows and movies, and play them as they are when the device can, with each person's own resume points, watched list and row of things to resume.
- Search finds titles in your library and the stations airing a match right now.
- Programs played this way count toward the limit on devices watching at once.
- For now, on your home network or through a VPN.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.21.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.21.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.21.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
