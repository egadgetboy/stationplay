StationPlay's apps can now browse and play your library.

## New

- **Your library in the apps.** The apps browse and search your shows and movies, and play each file without converting it when the device can.
- **Your own progress.** Each person has their own resume points, watched list and row of titles to resume.
- **Search.** Search finds titles in your library and the stations airing a match right now.

## Good to know

- **Off by default.** Choose which libraries to share on the **Access** tab, under **Your library in StationPlay's apps**. Stations, Plex, Jellyfin and other apps don't change.
- **Limits.** Programs played this way count toward the limit on devices watching at once. For now, it works on your home network or through a VPN.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.21.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.21.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.21.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
