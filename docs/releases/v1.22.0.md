**StationPlay's API.** `/api/v1` is now a documented, versioned API for your own scripts, home automation and players: the server, its stations, the guide and streams, its status, and Admin actions (update a station from Plex, check a station's files, ask Plex to refresh its guide, make a backup). Its OpenAPI spec is at `/api/v1/openapi.json`. Version 1 only grows: a change that would break a client becomes version 2, and the old address then carries `Deprecation` and `Sunset` headers for at least six months. See [docs/api.md](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay/blob/v1.22.0/docs/api.md).

**API tokens,** made on the **Access** tab: Viewer (reads only) or Admin, shown once and kept only as a hash, with an expiry or none, and revocable at any time. They work only with the API, and are refused from the internet unless an Admin turns that on, and then only over HTTPS.

**Versions of a title.** A title in 4K and 1080p, say, is listed once. Each device plays the best version it can play as it is and its connection keeps up with, or the one chosen. When playing can't keep up even with the buffer, short tests find where the trouble is, and the app offers a smaller version from where you are, or switches to it on its own, as an Admin chooses for at home and away.

**Skip intro and Skip credits** for episodes, only where Plex's markers fit the file. Movies don't offer them.

**Changed:** what only StationPlay's apps use (signing in, connection tests, your library) moved from `/api/v1` to `/api/internal`, which isn't a public API. StationPlay's apps need 1.22.0 or newer.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.22.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
