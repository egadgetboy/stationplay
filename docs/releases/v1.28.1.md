This release stops anyone from setting a passcode for someone else.

## Fixed

- **No passcodes for others.** Anyone at a device can pick someone without a passcode on **Who's tuning in?**, but that sign-in can no longer set a passcode for them or choose No passcode.
- **Setting your own passcode.** First sign in on the device with your password or an invite code. In the apps, choose **Someone else is watching** in Options, then sign in by name.

## Good to know

- **Other sign-ins unchanged.** Signing in with a password, an invite code or a passcode works as before, and so does setting or removing anyone's passcode on the **Access** tab.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.28.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.28.1.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.28.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
