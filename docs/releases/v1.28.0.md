This release sets who appears on each device's **Who's tuning in?**, asks for a PIN after the first sign-in and lets people change their password in the apps.

## New

- **Devices at home.** People on **Devices at home** appear on **Who's tuning in?** on every linked device while it's at home, on your network or VPN. Away from home, a device lists only people who signed in on it or were chosen for it.
- **Showing someone away from home.** Under **Devices** next to their name on the **Access** tab, choose **All devices** or the devices they use away from home. **Selected devices** and **Only where they sign in** are unchanged.
- **A PIN at first sign-in.** After someone first signs in on a device with a password or invite code, the apps ask for a 4-digit PIN, or No PIN. It applies on every device and can be changed in Options.
- **Passwords in the apps.** People can change their password in the apps' Options. They stay signed in there, and their other sign-ins end.
- **Who can change their password.** **Can change their own password** on the **Access** tab turns this off for someone. It's on by default, including for people already added, and an Admin can always change their own.
- **A consistent page.** Panels, cards, dialogs, buttons and fields share one corner rounding.

## Good to know

- **Moved to Devices at home.** Everyone on **All devices** moves to **Devices at home**, and so does **New people show on**. The access log shows how many people moved.
- **No password or PIN.** Someone with neither shows only on devices at home or on selected devices.
- **Admins and PINs.** An Admin who has a PIN keeps it, because an Admin always needs a PIN or their password on a device others use. You can still set or remove anyone's PIN on the **Access** tab.
- **Updated apps.** The PIN question, and the PIN and password in Options, come with the apps' own updates.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.28.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.28.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.28.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
