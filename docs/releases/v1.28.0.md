**New: who appears where on Who's tuning in?, a passcode after the first sign-in, and changing your password in the apps.**

- **Devices at home.** On Who's tuning in?, someone on **Devices at home** is on the list of every linked device while it's at home (on your network, or through your VPN). Away from home, a device lists only the people who signed in on it, and those you chose it for, so a phone at a friend's no longer shows your whole household. It's the household's default now: with this update, everyone on **All devices** moves to Devices at home, and so does **New people show on** (the access log says how many people moved). To have someone show away from home too, choose **All devices** for them, or the devices they're on away from home, under **Devices** beside their name on the **Access** tab. **Selected devices** (such as a family iPad that travels) and **Only where they sign in** stay as they were. Someone with neither a password nor a PIN shows on devices at home, or selected devices, only.
- **A passcode after the first sign-in.** The first time someone signs in on a device with their password or an invite code, StationPlay's apps ask them to choose a 4-digit passcode for picking themselves on Who's tuning in?, or No passcode. Either holds on every device, and they can change it in the app's Options. An Admin who has a passcode keeps one, as an Admin always needs a passcode or their password on a device others use too. You still set or remove anyone's PIN on the **Access** tab.
- **Changing your password in the apps.** People can change their own password in the apps' Options, as on StationPlay's page, and stay signed in there; every other sign-in of theirs ends. **Can change their own password**, beside each person's **New password** on the **Access** tab, turns that off for someone, in the apps and on the page alike. It's on to start with, for everyone already added too, and an Admin can always change their own.
- **The page.** Every panel, card, dialog, button and field shares one rounding, and labels beside an icon sit centered on it.
- **In the apps.** The passcode question, and the passcode and password in Options, come with the apps' own updates.
