**A thinner progress line.**

- **Stations' on-now line.** On the **Stations** tab, how far into its program each station is shows as a thin line, with a small mark at the point of play in the text color, as in StationPlay's apps.
- **In the apps, updated alongside.** Thin progress lines everywhere, Media's player on a phone held upright with one set of controls (on the picture), and text a little smaller on phones and tablets. These come with the apps' own updates (StationPlay for Android 0.2.3).

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.27.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.27.1.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.27.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
