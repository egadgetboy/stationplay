Progress lines are now thinner on StationPlay's page and in its apps.

## New

- **Thin progress lines.** On the **Stations** tab, each station's progress is now a thin line with a small mark where playback is, as in the apps.

## Good to know

- **Updated apps.** Thin progress lines, Media's player with one set of controls on a phone held upright and slightly smaller text on phones and tablets come with the apps' own updates (StationPlay for Android 0.2.3).

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.27.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.27.1.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.27.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
