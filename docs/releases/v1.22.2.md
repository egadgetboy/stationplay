**A new look: StationPlay's teal.**

- The accent is now teal, on StationPlay's page and in its apps: a deeper teal in light mode, checked for contrast against each background. Warnings in the Logs tab keep a warning color of their own.
- A station without a logo to take colors from is now deep blue with teal, in its Intro Bumper and cards.
- In StationPlay's apps, the row of programs partway through is called **Resume**.

---

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.2.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.22.2.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.2` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
