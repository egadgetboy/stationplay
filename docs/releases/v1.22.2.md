StationPlay's page and apps have a new teal accent.

## New

- **Teal accent.** Light mode uses a deeper teal, checked for contrast against each background.
- **Warnings stand out.** Warnings on the **Logs** tab keep their own color.
- **Stations without a logo.** Their Intro Bumper and cards use deep blue with teal.
- **Resume row.** In the apps, the row of programs you're partway through is now called **Resume**.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.22.2.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.22.2.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.22.2` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
