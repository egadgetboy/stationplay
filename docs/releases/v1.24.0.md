**New: your library plays on every device, and is easier to browse.**

- **Every file plays.** When a device can't play a file as it is, StationPlay now makes a copy as it plays, and you can still skip anywhere in it. Most of the time the picture is kept exactly as it is and only the package (and the sound, if the device needs it) changes, which costs next to nothing: Matroska files on iPhone and iPad, and DTS or Dolby TrueHD sound on most devices. Otherwise the picture is converted, at up to 1080p in standard color (HDR is made ordinary, as stations do it). Converting takes a share of the server's processor, so at most 3 are converted at once.
- **Subtitles everywhere.** Subtitles a device can't show itself, such as Blu-ray picture subtitles on a Roku or iPhone, are drawn into the picture.
- **A smaller picture when it can't keep up.** When the connection can't keep up with any version of a title, the apps can ask for a smaller copy made to fit.
- **Browsing your library.** Narrow a library to a genre or to what you haven't watched, sort it A to Z, by recently added or by release date, and jump straight to a letter.
- **More on each title.** A show's or movie's page has its tagline, its cast and crew, and others like it.
- **In the apps.** StationPlay's apps, updated alongside, now call their two sections Stations and Media, open on whichever one you used last, and the Roku app has favorites and the last station.

Copies are for StationPlay's own apps, on your home network or through a VPN for now. Nothing changes for stations, Plex or Jellyfin.

**Files.** `stationplay-1.24.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`); `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` for everything else.

**Updating.** Unzip `stationplay-1.24.0.zip` over `src` first, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.24.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. **New install:** see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
