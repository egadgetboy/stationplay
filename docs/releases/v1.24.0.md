Your library now plays on every device in StationPlay's apps, and it's easier to browse.

## New

- **Every file plays.** When a device can't play a file unchanged, StationPlay makes a copy as it plays, and you can still skip anywhere.
- **Usually just repackaged.** Most copies keep the picture and only repackage the file, at next to no cost. That covers Matroska files on iPhone and iPad, and DTS or Dolby TrueHD sound on most devices.
- **Converted when needed.** Otherwise the picture is converted at up to 1080p in standard color, with HDR made standard as on stations. At most 3 copies are converted at once.
- **Subtitles everywhere.** Subtitles a device can't show, such as Blu-ray picture subtitles on a Roku or iPhone, are drawn into the picture.
- **A smaller picture when needed.** When the connection can't keep up with any version of a title, the apps can ask for a smaller copy.
- **Browsing and details.** Filter by genre or unwatched, sort A to Z, by recently added or by release date, and jump to a letter. A title's page adds its tagline, cast and crew, and similar titles.

## Good to know

- **Updated apps.** The apps now call their sections Stations and Media and open on whichever you used last. The Roku app adds favorites and the last station.
- **Home network for now.** Copies work on your home network or through a VPN for now. Nothing changes for stations, Plex or Jellyfin.

## Updating

**Files.** `stationplay-1.24.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`). `stationplay.yaml` is the app definition for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

First unzip `stationplay-1.24.0.zip` over `src`. Then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.24.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations and settings are kept. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).
